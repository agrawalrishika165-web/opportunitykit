About OpportunityKit

The problem

Internships, scholarships and competitions list their rules in different places and formats. Students cannot tell if they qualify or what to prepare.

How it works

Every opportunity becomes one checklist. Each requirement is compared with your details and gets one of three statuses: Match, Mismatch or Missing info. Missing info is never treated as a fail. The app asks you for it instead.

What is different

  • Clear visible rules, no hidden AI yes/no answer. Each status has a plain reason.
  • Missing information is separate from failing.
  • The Gap-to-Action Roadmap turns every gap into a next step.
  • You can add any opportunity yourself, so it is not limited to the examples.

Technology

HTML, CSS and JavaScript. A rule-based comparison runs in your browser. Your details stay on your device (browser storage). There is no backend and no login.

Honest notes

  • Sample opportunities are made up. They are not verified listings.
  • Premium is a demo preview. No real payment happens.
  • Features marked "Planned" are not built yet.
